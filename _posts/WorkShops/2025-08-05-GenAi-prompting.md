## GenAI Prompting Essentials – Key Notes

### Prompting Basics
- **Prompting**: Giving clear, specific instructions to AI for desired outputs (text, images, etc.).
- **Five-Step Prompt Design (T.C.R.E.I.)**:
  1. **Task**: Define what AI should do; specify persona and output format.
  2. **Context**: Add relevant details for better results.
  3. **References**: Provide examples.
  4. **Evaluate**: Check if output meets your needs.
  5. **Iterate**: Refine and improve prompts.
- **Mnemonic**: "Tiny Crabs Ride Enormous Iguanas"

### Iteration Methods
- Revisit framework (add context/persona).
- Break prompt into shorter sentences.
- Try different phrasing/analogies.
- Add constraints.
- **Mnemonic**: "Rahen Saves Tragic Idiots"

### Multimodal Prompting
- Use various input types (text, images, audio) and get diverse outputs.

### AI Tool Issues & Mitigation
- **Hallucinations**: Incorrect/inconsistent outputs.
- **Biases**: AI may reflect human biases.
- **Mitigation**: Always verify with a human ("human in the loop").

---

### Practical Applications

#### Everyday Work Tasks
- Use AI for emails, writing, etc.
- Be specific about tone, word choice, and provide references.

#### Data Analysis & Presentations
- Use AI for spreadsheet tasks, trend identification, etc.
- **Caution**: Avoid sharing sensitive company data.

---

### Advanced Prompting Techniques

- **Prompt Chaining**: Guide AI through a sequence of related prompts.
- **Chain of Thought Prompting**: Ask AI to explain reasoning step-by-step.
- **Tree of Thought Prompting**: Explore multiple reasoning paths for complex problems.
- **Pro Tip**: Combine chain and tree of thought for deeper reasoning.
- **Meta Prompting**: Use AI to help generate prompts.

#### AI Agents
- **Agent Sim**: Simulates scenarios (e.g., interviews). Key: Persona, Task, Context, Stop Role.
- **Agent X**: Provides expert feedback. Key: Persona, Context, Task, Stop Role, extra material.

**Guidelines for AI Agents:**
- Assign a persona.
- Provide context/scenario.
- Specify interaction types/rules.
- Set a stop phrase.
- Include feedback/improvement tool.
